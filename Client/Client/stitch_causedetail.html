Clean Water for 500 Families in Rajasthan

About This Mission

In the heart of the Thar Desert, water is more than a resource—it's a luxury that costs families hours of manual labor every day. The current water crisis has reached a critical stage, with groundwater levels depleting and existing wells contaminated with high fluoride content.

DonerHQ is partnering with Jal Jeevan Mission to deploy high-precision solar-powered filtration systems that will serve 500 households. This project isn't just about digging wells; it's about building a sustainable, tech-monitored infrastructure that ensures long-term health and economic stability for the region.
